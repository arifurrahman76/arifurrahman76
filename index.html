01 / ABOUT

Sample to surveillance report

Clinical and laboratory work in poultry health.

Veterinarian and researcher with clinical and laboratory experience in poultry disease investigation, molecular diagnostics, bacteriology and antimicrobial susceptibility testing. I investigate poultry respiratory pathogens, characterise bacterial isolates and contribute to animal disease surveillance. My research also spans infectious disease epidemiology, microbial genomics and quantitative analysis in R.

I am seeking advanced residency training in poultry medicine and diagnostics to strengthen diagnostic case interpretation, flock health assessment and evidence-based disease prevention, with the long-term goal of contributing to poultry health and diagnostic services in Bangladesh.
